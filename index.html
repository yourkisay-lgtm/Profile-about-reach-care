About the System

What is REACH-CARE?

Remote Evaluation and Accessible Clinic Healthcare Information System is a digital healthcare platform designed to improve healthcare access in remote communities.

Our Mission

Bringing community healthcare closer to home.

REACH-CARE is designed to connect residents in remote barangays with municipal healthcare professionals and provide practical digital tools for local care teams.

◎

Our Purpose

Make basic healthcare consultations more accessible by connecting residents with municipal doctors and supporting local health workers with digital tools.

↗

The Problem We Solve

Long travel distances, expensive transportation, paper records, and delayed information can make care difficult. REACH-CARE aims to help reduce these barriers.

♥

Community first

The system is intended to support—not replace—patients, barangay health workers, doctors, and local health services.

Responsible Care and Information Use

Policies and Ethical Guidelines

These are the REACH-CARE project's guidelines for responsible handling of personal and health information. They reflect the system's intended use and do not certify legal compliance or institutional approval.

◇

Data Privacy and Protection

Collect and use personal and health information only for legitimate, clearly explained system and care purposes. REACH-CARE aims to respect the privacy principles of the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173); this statement is not a certification of compliance. The system owner must confirm applicable obligations and safeguards.

⌑

Confidentiality of Medical Records

Consultation details, prescriptions, records, and personal information should be accessed only by authorized users for an authorized purpose and must not be disclosed without proper authority. The application uses role checks and limits patient-facing views to the signed-in patient's own information.

i

Informed Consent

Patients should be told what information is collected, why it is used, and how to ask questions before providing it. Consent should be obtained when required. The current application does not include a dedicated consent-recording workflow; the responsible organization must establish and communicate the appropriate notice and consent process.

♡

Respect for Patients

Treat every patient with dignity, fairness, and respect. Provide access and assistance without discrimination, and respect each person's decisions and rights throughout care and use of the system.

+

Patient Safety and Beneficence

Use REACH-CARE to support patient well-being, care coordination, and communication. It does not replace qualified professional judgment, in-person assessment, or emergency medical services. Doctors and healthcare workers remain responsible for their professional decisions and actions.

✓

Transparency and Accountability

Explain how information is used and limit access to authorized work. Users are responsible for the accuracy and appropriateness of their actions. The application does not currently include a built-in audit log, so operational monitoring and incident response must be established by the responsible organization.

≋

Data Accuracy and Integrity

Enter information carefully and keep records accurate and current. Changes should be made only by authorized personnel and must not intentionally falsify or misrepresent information. Patients who need profile corrections should contact the system administrator; the application currently has no self-service profile editing.

Safeguards and operational responsibilities

The application uses hashed passwords, prepared database statements, role checks, patient-scoped patient views, output escaping, and CSRF checks on protected forms. These code-level controls do not by themselves establish secure hosting, encrypted transmission or storage, protected backups, staff training, access reviews, or a retention and deletion process. The system owner must verify and maintain those operational safeguards and provide users with an approved privacy contact and process for questions or concerns.
